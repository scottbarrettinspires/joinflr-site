LeadStream Systems

Lead generation and conversion, exclusively at FLR

Turn everything you do into a lead funnel

Most lead pages send a visitor to one call to action and hope. LeadStream Systems works the other way around: leads get to know, like and trust you first, then they call. It is a lead generation and conversion system you do not have to build yourself, ready to put to work as you start your career.

Every sign, flyer, post card, business card and social post carries a code that sends the right person to the right mobile page. Leads call you directly and feed your CRM automatically, so nothing waits and nothing is forgotten.
